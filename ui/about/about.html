About TruWheels

Building confidence in every used car purchase.

Why TruWheels Was Built

Buying a car is a dream for many people, but buying a used car often comes with uncertainty, hidden problems and misinformation.

Most first-time buyers do not possess the technical knowledge required to properly evaluate a vehicle or determine whether a deal is truly worth it.

TruWheels was created to bridge this gap by providing buyers with professional-grade intelligence, helping them make informed and confident decisions before purchasing a used car.

Our Mission

To empower every used car buyer with transparent, unbiased and easy-to-understand vehicle intelligence.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted used car intelligence platform and ensure that no buyer purchases a vehicle without complete confidence.

Built By

TruWheels is an independent initiative founded and developed by Divyansh Chaudhary.

Started in 2026, TruWheels reflects a commitment to solving real-world problems through technology and making used car buying safer for Indian consumers.
